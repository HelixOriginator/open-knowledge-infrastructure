# Open Knowledge Infrastructure Initiative

A public-interest initiative dedicated to democratizing knowledge production through open, accessible, and independently produced research infrastructure.

## Overview

The Open Knowledge Infrastructure Initiative is a mission-driven effort founded by Kallol Chakrabarti to explore how independent researchers can build, preserve, and scale open-access knowledge systems outside traditional institutional structures.

The initiative is built upon a growing ecosystem of DOI-linked research outputs, datasets, frameworks, reports, publications, presentations, and public-interest research assets distributed across global repositories and scholarly platforms.

This website serves as a funding and partnership platform for foundations, philanthropists, CSR programs, research supporters, and organizations interested in strengthening open knowledge ecosystems.

---

## Mission

To expand equitable access to knowledge by building sustainable, open, and publicly accessible research infrastructure that supports independent scholarship, public-interest research, and evidence-based decision-making.

---

## Why This Matters

Research production remains heavily concentrated within resource-intensive institutions.

Independent researchers, citizen scholars, and practitioners often face barriers to participation despite possessing valuable expertise and local knowledge.

This initiative explores alternative models of knowledge production that are:

* Open Access
* Publicly Verifiable
* DOI-Linked
* Globally Discoverable
* Community-Oriented
* Public Benefit Focused

## Research Architecture

A key framework developed through this initiative is:

### The Kallol Taxonomy of Research DOIs

The taxonomy categorizes research outputs into:

1. Repository-Based Outputs
2. Dataset Outputs
3. Formal Publication Outputs

The objective is to improve discoverability, organization, and understanding of diverse research objects within modern open scholarship ecosystems.

## Strategic Objectives

### Preservation

Ensure long-term accessibility and sustainability of existing research assets.

### Scaling

Expand research capacity, open datasets, and public-interest knowledge resources.

### Institutionalization

Develop sustainable structures that allow the initiative to operate as a long-term public resource beyond any single individual.
